scviva-tools block diagram

Architecture map for the scviva-tools package: upstream scvi-tools inheritance, owned spatial foundations, and two parallel tracks — transcriptomics models (SCVIVA, ResolVI, DestVI, GIMVI, Stereoscope, Tangram) and imaging models (ImagingBaseModel → SPARL). Both tracks share SpatialBaseModel.

Two parallel tracks both inherit from SpatialBaseModel. Transcriptomics track: VAE-based models trained end-to-end. Imaging track: pre-trained ViT backbones loaded via from_pretrained() — no SSL pre-training in scviva-tools (v1 inference-only).
